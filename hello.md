Step 1: Proportional (Kp)
Set Ki = 0, Kd = 0
Increase Kp until system oscillates
Reduce Kp to 60-70% of oscillation point
Step 2: Integral (Ki)
Slowly increase Ki to eliminate steady-state error
Stop when system becomes unstable
Reduce Ki if overshoot is too large
Step 3: Derivative (Kd)
Add small amount of Kd to reduce overshoot
Increase until settling time improves
Avoiding excessive Kd (amplifies noise)
